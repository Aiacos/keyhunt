<objective>
Move all ~123 shared global variable definitions from keyhunt.cpp to a dedicated src/globals.h + src/globals.cpp module. This resolves Gap 2 (mode_bsgs.cpp 51 externs to keyhunt.cpp) and partially resolves Gap 1 (removes ~240 lines of global definitions from keyhunt.cpp).

Moving definitions to globals.cpp makes them a shared resource that no single file "owns," eliminating the architectural anti-pattern.

Output: src/globals.h (struct definitions + extern declarations), src/globals.cpp (variable definitions), updated keyhunt.cpp (~240 lines removed), updated mode_bsgs.cpp (51 inline externs replaced by single #include).
